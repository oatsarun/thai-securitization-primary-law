# Thai securitization: primary law set

Primary legal texts relied on in a review of a research memo on securitizing a Thai car-loan receivable pool. Public instruments only. Assembled September 2026.

Start with `SECURITIZATION-MAP.pdf` (or the `.html`): every instrument here, the sections that matter, what each does and which part of the memo it bears on. Tags such as A2, D3 or G6 are the memo's own section labels.

## Layout

- `pdf/` the instruments as published by the issuing body: the Council of State's consolidated texts for the Emergency Decree on Special Purpose Juristic Persons for Securitization B.E. 2540, the Trust for Transactions in Capital Market Act B.E. 2550 and the Civil and Commercial Code, the Royal Gazette copy of the 2015 amending Act and of the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, a complete consolidated text of the Bankruptcy Act B.E. 2483, the Revenue Department's texts of the Royal Decrees and Director-General's notifications issued under the Revenue Code, the SEC's notifications, the Bank of Thailand's and the Consumer Protection Board's instruments. Filenames give instrument, year and subject.
- `html/` four instruments the Revenue Department and the SEC publish only as web pages, captured as HTML: two Departmental Orders, one Board of Taxation ruling and one SEC Office notification.
- `text/` a plain-text mirror of each file, with a header recording source, fetch date and the sha256 of the PDF it mirrors. Three PDFs are scans with no text layer and their mirrors say so.
- `images/` page images of the operative sections, cropped from the instruments, including the sections of the Civil and Commercial Code and the Revenue Code that the review cites.

## Not included

The full consolidated Revenue Code. It is a general code available from the Revenue Department (rd.go.th); the sections relied on are reproduced as page images in `images/`.

The Civil and Commercial Code, the Bankruptcy Act and the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, by contrast, are included in full as consolidated texts: the Civil and Commercial Code as the Office of the Council of State's consolidated text (current through amendment No. 25 B.E. 2568), the Bankruptcy Act as a complete consolidated text (to amendment No. 10 B.E. 2561) and the PDPA as the complete Royal Gazette original. A further, partial Council of State print of the PDPA (through s.72 only) is included alongside the complete copy and is marked as partial in its own filename and header.

Nothing in this set is legal advice; Thai text governs, and the SEC's English translation of the Trust Act is marked unofficial by the SEC itself.
